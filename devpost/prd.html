Contract Health Scoring Engine

Calculated dynamically across four critical freelancer risk pillars:

✓ Payment Terms: Penalizes Net-60/90, pay-if-paid, or ambiguous milestone signoffs.
✓ Scope & Revisions: Penalizes unlimited revisions or lifetime uncompensated defect fixing.
✓ Intellectual Property: Penalizes clauses seizing pre-existing developer tooling or personal portfolios.
✓ Restrictive Covenants: Penalizes overbroad non-competes barring the freelancer from working in their industry.

Diplomatic Action: The Negotiation Engine

Provides tailored, polite counter-clauses and email copy so freelancers push back with zero awkwardness:

"Hi [Client], I reviewed the draft agreement and am excited to collaborate. Regarding Section 7, standard industry practice provides a 30-day post-launch warranty period for deliverables, after which ongoing support is billed at our standard hourly rate. Would this revised wording work for you?"
