Freelance Contract & Scope-Creep Sentinel — Proof of Concept Scope

💡 The Unique Kernel

Awareness + Diplomatic Action. Beyond flagging risky legal clauses, FairShare arms the freelancer with polite, professional, ready-to-copy counter-clauses and email templates so they can push back safely without damaging client goodwill.

👤 Target User

Independent & early-career freelancers (devs, designers, creators) reviewing contracts without a lawyer, vulnerable to predatory terms like unpaid bug fixes, Net-90 delays, and IP traps.

🎯 Visual Identity

Clean, executive SaaS craftsmanship (Linear / Stripe style). Professional dark theme, crisp typography, and clear risk badges (Green / Amber / Crimson) with zero toy or cartoonish elements.

Project Flow & Core Loop

IN SCOPE Client-Side PDF & Text Parser: Fast file drop or 1-click sample contract loading for instant judge testing.
IN SCOPE Fairness Score Engine: Immediate 0–100 rating with clear risk thresholds (Healthy, Caution, Critical).
IN SCOPE Key Trap Detection: Flags unpaid post-delivery fixes, Net-90 delays, sneaky IP grabs, and broad non-competes.
IN SCOPE Diplomatic Counter-Clauses: Pre-crafted, polite pushback templates ready to copy into an email with 1 click.
LATER User Accounts & Cloud History: Save and compare past scanned contracts across client projects.
LATER Version Comparison / Diff Viewer: Side-by-side comparison of original contract vs. client revisions.
LATER Tone Adjustment Sliders: Dynamically modulate pushback tone from "Collaborative" to "Strict Formal Legal".
CUT Full Legal E-Signing: Digital signature workflows require complex third-party certificate infrastructure not needed to prove the sentinel concept.
CUT Paid Lawyer Marketplace: Connecting users to live legal attorneys distracts from the autonomous developer tool focus.
